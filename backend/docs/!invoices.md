# Invoices

Create a professional invoice, send it, and track payment without leaving Itemize.

## Create and send

1. Open **Invoices** and start a new invoice.
2. Choose a client, add line items, and set dates.
3. Add **Notes** for terms your customer should see.
4. Use **Footer** for a thank-you line or tax note. That text appears on the preview and PDF.
5. Save, then send or download the PDF.

## Tax

Use **Add tax** for an invoice-level rate (for example 11% on $5,000 is $550). The editor keeps that rate when you reopen the invoice.

## Payments

Record payments as they come in. The invoice shows amount due, paid, and overdue at a glance.
