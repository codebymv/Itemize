# Billing

Plans are billed per organization. Upgrade anytime from Settings.

## Solo

For freelancers needing signature, invoicing, and organization tools. Includes the full Workspace, contacts and pipelines, invoices, 25 e-signatures per month, inbox, forms, landing pages, and three teammates. A user who owns a live Solo organization may own up to three organizations total.

## Studio

Everything in Solo, plus unlimited e-signatures, higher contact and teammate limits, automations, priority support, and no organization-ownership cap.

## Organization ownership and billing

Billing and paid features belong to each organization. A live plan on any organization
you own sets how many organizations you may own: Free allows one, Solo allows three,
and Studio allows unlimited. Additional organizations start on Free and must be
upgraded separately to use paid features.

Transferring ownership keeps the organization's subscription with that organization.
The new owner must have room under the allowance that would apply after the
transfer. A downgrade or expired trial never deletes an existing organization; it
prevents creating or accepting ownership of another organization while the owner
is at or above the new limit.

## Usage

Settings shows usage for emails, signatures, and related caps. Meters stay visible even at 0%.

## Yearly billing

Yearly is billed up front and saves about 17% versus month-to-month.
