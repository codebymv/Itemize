# Billing

Plans are billed per workspace. Upgrade anytime from Settings.

## Solo

For freelancers needing signature, invoicing, and organization tools. Includes the full workspace, contacts and pipelines, invoices, 25 e-signatures per month, inbox, forms, landing pages, and three teammates. A user who owns a live Solo workspace may own up to three workspaces total.

## Studio

Everything in Solo, plus unlimited e-signatures, higher contact and teammate limits, automations, priority support, and no workspace-ownership cap.

## Workspace ownership and billing

Billing and paid features belong to each workspace. A live plan on any workspace
you own sets how many workspaces you may own: Free allows one, Solo allows three,
and Studio allows unlimited. Additional workspaces start on Free and must be
upgraded separately to use paid features.

Transferring ownership keeps the workspace's subscription with that workspace.
The new owner must have room under the allowance that would apply after the
transfer. A downgrade or expired trial never deletes an existing workspace; it
prevents creating or accepting ownership of another workspace while the owner
is at or above the new limit.

## Usage

Settings shows usage for emails, signatures, and related caps. Meters stay visible even at 0%.

## Yearly billing

Yearly is billed up front and saves about 17% versus month-to-month.
