# Getting Started with Itemize

Itemize is a workspace for organizing work, invoicing clients, and collecting signatures — without stacking extra tools.

## What you can do

- **Workspace** — lists, notes, and whiteboards on a canvas
- **Invoices** — create, tax, send, and track payment
- **Signatures** — send documents to sign in the browser
- **Sharing** — publish a list, note, or whiteboard with a link

Solo is for freelancers needing signature, invoicing, and organization tools. Studio adds unlimited signatures, higher limits, and automations. See [Billing](/help/!billing) for details.
