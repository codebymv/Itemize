# Signatures

Send a document for e-signature and follow it until it is signed.

## Send a document

Open **Documents** (or start from an invoice when you need a signature with billing). Add the file, place signature fields, and send it to your client.

## Limits

- **Solo** includes 25 e-signatures per month.
- **Studio** includes unlimited e-signatures.

Upgrade from Settings if you hit the Solo cap.

## After you send

Track status from the documents list. Your client signs in the browser — no extra account required for them.
