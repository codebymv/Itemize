# Sharing

Share lists, notes, whiteboards, and vaults with a link — without giving someone your whole workspace.

## Public links

Open an item, choose share, and copy the public link. Anyone with the link can view the shared item.

## Shared page

**Shared** in the sidebar lists items you have already published. Unshare from there or from the item itself.

## What stays private

Contacts, invoices, and deals are not shared by a canvas link. Share only the list, note, whiteboard, or vault you selected.
